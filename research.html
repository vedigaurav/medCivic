Research

Exploring the intersection of medicine, technology and public policy

Home Research

Digital Health & Patient Safety

Evaluating digital health tools, telehealth platforms, and medical apps for safety, accuracy, and equity impacts on patients and providers.

Privacy, PII & Health Data Security

Investigating how personal health information is collected, shared, and protected across platforms, apps, and public health systems.

Online Health Fraud & DNS Abuse

Studying deceptive health websites, counterfeit pharmacy domains, and DNS-layer abuse that exposes patients to unsafe products and misinformation.

AI & Medicine

Examining the ethical, regulatory, and clinical implications of artificial intelligence in diagnostics, treatment recommendations, and healthcare delivery.

Internet Governance & Public Policy

Analyzing global policy frameworks, domain name governance, and regulatory approaches that affect health information access and online safety.

Research & Impact Reports

Publishing accessible, evidence-based reports that translate our findings into actionable policy recommendations for regulators, providers, and the public.

Our research informs real-world solutions for a healthier and more trustworthy Internet.

Research Partners

MedCivic collaborates with academic institutions, civil society organizations, and domain name industry bodies to amplify our research impact.

Academic Institutions
